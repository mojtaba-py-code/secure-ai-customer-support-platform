# Acme SoundBar 500 Setup and Troubleshooting

## Connecting to your TV

Connect the SoundBar 500 to your TV's HDMI port labelled ARC or eARC with the included HDMI cable. Turn on HDMI-CEC (sometimes called Anynet+, Bravia Sync or SimpLink) in your TV settings so the TV remote controls the soundbar volume. If your TV has no ARC port, use the optical cable instead.

## Pairing the subwoofer

The SoundBar 500 pairs automatically with the Acme Subwoofer 200. If the subwoofer LED blinks red, press and hold the pairing button on the back of the subwoofer for 5 seconds, then press the Bluetooth button on the soundbar remote.

## No sound

1. Check that the TV's audio output is set to "HDMI ARC" or "External speaker".
2. Press the Input button on the remote until the front display shows ARC (or OPT for optical).
3. Unplug the soundbar for 30 seconds and plug it back in.

## Sound is out of sync with the picture

Set your TV's digital audio output to "PCM" instead of "Auto" or "Bitstream", then adjust the AV sync setting in the soundbar menu.

## Resetting to factory settings

Press and hold the Power and Volume Down buttons on the soundbar at the same time for 10 seconds until the display shows RESET.
