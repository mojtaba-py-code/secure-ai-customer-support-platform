# Acme SmartCam 2 Setup and Troubleshooting

## Before you start

The SmartCam 2 connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only. If your router uses one network name for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, temporarily move closer to the router during setup.

## Setup

1. Install the Acme Home app and sign in.
2. Plug in the camera; the status light blinks blue when it is ready to pair.
3. In the app choose Add device > SmartCam 2 and follow the steps.

## The camera will not connect

- Make sure you selected a 2.4 GHz network and typed the Wi-Fi password correctly.
- Restart your router and the camera.
- Reset the camera: press and hold the reset button next to the USB-C port for 10 seconds until the light turns solid red, then set it up again.

## Night vision is blurry

Clean the lens with a soft cloth and make sure there is no glass (for example a window) in front of the camera; infrared light reflects off glass.

## Firmware updates

The camera updates itself overnight when an update is available. Keep it plugged in and connected.
