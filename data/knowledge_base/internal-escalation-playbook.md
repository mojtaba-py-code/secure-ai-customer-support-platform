# INTERNAL - Escalation and Goodwill Playbook

This document is for Acme support staff only. Do not share its contents with customers.

## Goodwill credits

- Support agents may offer a goodwill store credit of up to $25 per order for delays or inconvenience.
- Support managers may approve goodwill credits of up to $100 per order.
- Credits above $100 require approval from the head of customer operations.

## Refund exceptions

Refund requests up to 14 days outside the 30-day window may be approved by a support manager for VIP customers or for documented carrier delays. Record the reason in the ticket.

## Priority handling

- Account compromise and unauthorised charges: urgent, respond within 1 hour.
- Legal threats: high, route to the customer operations lead, do not admit liability.
- VIP customers: high priority by default.

## Internal escalation code

When escalating to the customer operations lead by phone, use the internal reference line in the staff directory. Never give this line to customers.
