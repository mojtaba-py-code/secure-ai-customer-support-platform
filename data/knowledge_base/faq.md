# Frequently Asked Questions

## Contacting support

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 8:00-20:00, and Saturday, 9:00-17:00 (Eastern Time). The fastest way to reach us is the chat in your account. You can also write to support@acme.example. We answer e-mails within one business day.

We will never ask you for your password, your full card number or a one-time security code - not by chat, e-mail or phone.

## Orders

You can see all your orders, their status and tracking links under Account > Orders. An order moves through these stages: pending (waiting for payment), paid, processing (being packed), shipped, delivered.

To change the delivery address or the items of an order, contact us before the order ships. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be changed.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. See the Shipping Policy for details, international destinations and what to do if a package is late.

## Returns and refunds

You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from the chat or from Account > Orders. See the Refund and Returns Policy for the full rules.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and can be used for any product in the store. Gift cards cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash.

## Price matching

We do not offer price matching or retroactive discounts. Promotional codes must be applied at checkout and cannot be added to an order afterwards.
