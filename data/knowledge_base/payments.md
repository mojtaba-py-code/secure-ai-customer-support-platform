# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, bank transfer and Acme gift cards.

## When you are charged

When you place an order, your card is authorised for the order total. The payment is captured when the order is processed for shipping. You will see only one charge per order.

## Seeing two charges for one order

A pending authorisation and the final charge can briefly both appear on your statement. The authorisation disappears automatically, usually within 3-5 business days. If you still see two completed charges for the same order after that, contact us with the order number and we will investigate.

## Declined or failed payments

A payment can fail when the card issuer declines it, the card has expired, there are insufficient funds, or your bank requires extra verification (3-D Secure) that was not completed. Orders with a failed payment stay pending for 48 hours so you can retry with the same or a different payment method; after that they are cancelled automatically.

## Invoices

An invoice is attached to your shipping confirmation e-mail and is also available under Account > Orders.

## Security

Card details are processed by our payment provider. We only store the card brand and the last four digits so you can recognise the card.
