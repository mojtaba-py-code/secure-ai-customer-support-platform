# Privacy Notice (Summary)

## What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account and place orders (name, e-mail address, phone number, delivery addresses), your order and payment history (card brand and last four digits only), and your support conversations.

## How we use it

We use your information to deliver orders, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Support conversations may be processed by an AI assistant; personal details that the assistant does not need are removed before processing.

## How long we keep it

Support conversations are kept for 24 months. Order and invoice records are kept for 7 years as required by tax law.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@acme.example or ask the support team in chat. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.
