# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling before shipment

You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is pending, paid or processing - that is, before it has shipped. Ask the assistant in chat or use Account > Orders. For your protection you always confirm the cancellation yourself in the app.

## What happens to your payment

- If your payment was only authorised, the authorisation is released. Most banks remove it within 3-5 business days.
- If your payment was already captured, we refund the full amount to your original payment method automatically when the cancellation is confirmed.

## After an order has shipped

Shipped orders cannot be cancelled. Once the order is delivered you can return it within 30 days under the Refund and Returns Policy.
