# Acme Mesh Router X3 Troubleshooting

## Slow or dropping Wi-Fi

1. Place the satellite units no more than two rooms away from the main unit, off the floor and away from microwaves and large metal objects.
2. In the Acme Home app, run Network > Optimise. This picks the least congested channels.
3. Restart the main unit, wait two minutes, then restart the satellites.

## A satellite shows a red light

The satellite lost its connection to the main unit. Move it closer to the main unit and wait five minutes. If the light stays red, press the reset button on the satellite for 10 seconds and add it again in the app.

## No internet on any device

Restart your modem first, then the main router unit. Check in the app whether the internet connection shows "Connected". If your provider requires a username and password (PPPoE), enter them under Network > Internet.

## Factory reset

Press and hold the reset button on the main unit for 15 seconds until the light blinks white. All settings, including your Wi-Fi name and password, are erased.
