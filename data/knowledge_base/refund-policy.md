# Refund and Returns Policy

## Refund window

You can request a refund for a delivered order within 30 days of the delivery date. The refund window starts on the day the carrier marks the order as delivered.

Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded; if you no longer want an order that has not shipped, cancel it instead (see the Order Cancellation Policy).

## Condition of returned items

Items should be returned in their original packaging with all accessories. Opened items are accepted if they are complete and undamaged. Damaged or defective items are always accepted within the refund window.

## Items that cannot be refunded

- Gift cards
- Software licence keys that have been revealed
- Items damaged through misuse after delivery

## How to request a refund

1. Ask the assistant in chat or open Account > Orders and choose "Request refund".
2. Review the request and confirm it. Nothing is refunded until you confirm.
3. A member of our team reviews every refund request, usually within 2 business days.
4. After approval, the refund is sent to your original payment method.

## When you receive your money

Approved refunds are processed within 1 business day. Your bank or card issuer usually shows the refund within 5-7 business days. PayPal refunds usually appear within 1 business day.

## Partial refunds

If only some items of an order are returned, or an item is returned incomplete, we refund the value of the returned items. The total of all refunds on an order can never exceed the amount you paid.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, please tell us within 7 days of delivery and include a photo if you can. We will arrange a free return and send a replacement or a full refund.
