# Limited Warranty

## Warranty period

All Acme products come with a 12-month limited warranty from the date of delivery. Products of the Pro line (televisions and the Mesh Router X3) have a 24-month warranty.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, for example a device that does not power on, a speaker that distorts at normal volume or a camera that fails to connect despite following the setup guide.

## What is not covered

- Damage from drops, liquids, power surges or unauthorised repairs
- Normal wear such as scratches or battery capacity decreasing over time
- Consumables and accessories sold separately

## How to make a warranty claim

Contact support with your order number and the product's serial number (printed on the label under the device). We may ask you to try a few troubleshooting steps first. If the product is defective we repair it, replace it with the same or an equivalent model, or refund it if neither is possible.

Warranty repairs and replacements are free, including shipping both ways.
