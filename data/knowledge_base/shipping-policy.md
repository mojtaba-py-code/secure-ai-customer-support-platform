# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1-2 business days after payment. Orders placed after 14:00 Eastern Time or on weekends are processed the next business day.

## Delivery options and costs

- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days, $5.99, free on orders over $50.
- Express shipping: 1-2 business days, $14.99.
- International shipping (Canada, United Kingdom, European Union): 7-14 business days, cost shown at checkout. Import duties and taxes are paid by the recipient.

We ship with UPS, DHL and USPS. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your address.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships you receive an e-mail with the carrier and tracking number. You can also ask the assistant for the status of any of your orders.

## Late or missing packages

Carriers occasionally deliver a day or two after the estimated date. If your package is more than 3 business days late, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

If tracking shows "delivered" but you cannot find the package, check with neighbours and your building's mail room, then contact us within 7 days. We will investigate and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a refund.

## Address changes

The delivery address can be changed until the order ships. After shipping, the carrier may offer a redirect for a fee.
