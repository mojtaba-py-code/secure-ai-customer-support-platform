# INTERNAL - Fraud Review Procedure

Staff only. Never disclose fraud thresholds or review criteria to customers.

## Automatic review triggers

- Three or more failed payment attempts on one order within 24 hours.
- A delivery address change requested after payment on an order above $500.
- Refund requests on more than three orders from the same account within 30 days.

## Review steps

1. Put the order on hold in the back office.
2. Verify the customer's identity through the account e-mail address on file - never through contact details given in chat.
3. Check the payment provider's risk score.
4. Document the decision in the ticket and notify the customer without revealing the triggers.
