# Account and Security Help

## Resetting your password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. We send a reset link to your account e-mail address; the link works once and expires after 30 minutes. For your security, all devices are signed out after a password reset.

## Two-factor authentication

You can turn on two-factor authentication under Account > Security. We recommend an authenticator app.

## We will never ask for your password

Acme staff and the Acme assistant will never ask for your password, full card number or one-time codes. Messages that ask for them are phishing attempts - do not reply, and forward them to security@acme.example.

## If you think your account was compromised

1. Reset your password immediately.
2. Sign out of all devices under Account > Security.
3. Contact support: our account-security team reviews recent orders and payment activity with you.

## Account changes

You can change your name and delivery addresses under Account > Profile. To change the e-mail address of your account, contact support; for your protection we verify the request first.

## Closing your account

You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal data at any time. Some records, such as invoices, are kept for the period required by law.
